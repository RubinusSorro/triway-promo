Getting a tricycle shouldn’t take this much effort

Tricycles are part of daily life in Antipolo, but finding one is not always easy for students and workers.

Long walks

The nearest terminal can be far from home, school, or work.

Long waits

You wait for a ride to become available, with no idea when it will arrive.

Unclear fares

You often don’t know how much you’ll pay until the trip is over.

How TRIWAY works

Request a ride, then follow it from start to finish on the same screen your driver uses.

  1. SearchingYou book a route and see the fare.
  2. AcceptedA TODA driver accepts your request.
  3. ArrivingYour driver heads to your pickup point.
  4. OngoingYour trip is underway.
  5. CompletedYou pay in cash and the trip is saved.

Built for riders and drivers

TRIWAY works with traditional terminals and TODAs. It adds a flexible way to book, not a replacement.

For commuters

  • Book a tricycle without walking to a terminal
  • See the estimated fare before you confirm
  • Choose One-Way or Round Trip
  • View routes on a map
  • Track your ride and review past trips

For TODAs and drivers

  • Switch availability on or off
  • Receive and accept ride requests
  • Update trip progress step by step
  • Reach more passengers within your service area
  • Keep a history of completed trips
